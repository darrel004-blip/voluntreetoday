01 — UNDERSTANDING VOLUNTEERING

Be part of
something good.

Volunteering means freely giving your time, energy, or skills to support others and contribute to the common good. The goal is to help—not to receive financial rewards.

It connects people, builds trust, and turns shared concerns into positive action.

Explore the UN Volunteers definition ↗
WHY IT MATTERS

Good for others.
Good for you, too.

Volunteering creates shared value. It can respond to real needs while helping volunteers learn, connect, and grow.

volunteering.

Organised volunteering is coordinated by a school, foundation, charity, community group, or non-profit organisation. Teams plan activities, share responsibilities, and work toward a common goal.

YOUR FIRST STEP

Make your good
intention practical.

A thoughtful volunteer is prepared, respectful, and ready to learn.

01

Choose a cause

Think about the community or issue you care about.

02

Find a trusted organiser

Check the organisation, activity details, and how donations or support are used.

03

Ask, prepare, and participate

Follow instructions, respect the people you support, and work within your skills.

04

Reflect and keep contributing

Learn from the experience and look for safe ways to stay involved.
